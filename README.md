### 👩‍💻 About Me

> **"I engineer high-performance mobile and web applications that solve complex business and legal challenges at scale."**

I am a product-focused **Mobile & Software Engineer** with extensive experience in architecting and delivering enterprise-grade mobile applications. Most notably, I have contributed to core production features for **Taxmann Store** (India’s flagship legal & tax publication platform) and **Centax Online** (comprehensive tax intelligence and research engine), trusted daily by thousands of Chartered Accountants, corporate firms, and legal professionals across India.

- 🎯 **Domain Focus:** Cross-platform mobile development, predictable Redux state flows, sub-second query rendering, and secure e-commerce pipelines.
- 🚀 **Engineering Philosophy:** Writing clean, testable, and modular code with high performance, seamless 60 FPS transitions, and robust crash resilience.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** React Native, Redux Architecture, Firebase Services, RESTful API Integration, and Responsive Mobile UI/UX.
